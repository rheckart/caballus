# An Organisation chooses its Features, and a gate it switches off is not an override

Caballus was designed for one rescue, and ADR 0030 made it serve several. Until now every rescue it served had the same shape. The first prospect that does not is a working farm. It also runs a dead animal recovery service, so some of its work happens off the farm. Its owner sketched the app he wants, and the sketch is almost nothing but a chore list: _Water horses_, _Water cattle_, _Feed pigs_, _Feed horses across road_, _Let chickens out and water_, _Charge dump trailers_, _Local DAR_. Each line covers the whole farm. A photograph stands in for a finished chore. One worker does the job, often paid. A _Boss_ who is not on the job is one tap away by call or text.

Nothing in that sketch needs an Orientation, a Release, a Consent, Medication Authority, a Lead, a Reading, a Days of Supply count, a Board or a sign-in sheet. Each of those is either a hard gate that would refuse the farm's own workers, or a screen it would never open. Several of them are hard gates **with no override**, on purpose (ADR 0011, ADR 0017). This ADR is mostly about how an Organisation can do without them without bringing back the override those ADRs rejected.

## The decision

**An Organisation has Settings, and Settings are grouped into Features.** A **Setting** is one named choice, such as _an Orientation is required before rostering_. The list of Settings is closed and lives in the code. Every Setting has a decided consequence for each derivation it touches: rosterability, the staffing facts, Cover, Shift Authority. Adding a Setting is a deploy. Changing a Setting's value is not. A **Feature** is a named group of Settings. When it is off, its screens go with it and the work it gated stops being gated. Nothing recorded under it is ever deleted. The first list is only what the first differing prospect needs off:

| Feature              | What switching it off means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Compliance           | Three independent Settings: Orientation required, Release required, and Consent required for a minor. With no Orientation requirement the Candidate state disappears: a Worker is rosterable from creation, and Cover needs an Account and nothing else. Each Setting brings the fact it depends on. _Consent required_ cannot lean on an Orientation having recorded a date of birth, so with it on, a Worker with no date of birth is not rosterable and the gap says so. |
| Medication Authority | Anyone rostered may tick a Medicate Item, and _nobody who can give medication_ is no longer a staffing fact. The dose is **still its own Item**: a missed dose folded into a feeding tick is the failure the separate Item exists to prevent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Leads                | Everyone rostered on a Shift holds Shift Authority over it, and _no Lead_ is no longer a staffing fact. That is the whole set: moving the start time, dropping Discretionary Work, declaring Short and texting about it, writing a Days of Supply reading, closing. `roster` still reaches every Shift, and that is how a Boss who is not on the job gets in.                                                                                                               |
| Weather              | No Readings and no Conditions. Its Organisation-wide Conditions need no horse records. Its per-horse Thresholds do.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Supplies             | No Days of Supply and no Reorders.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Board                | No kiosk.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Attendance           | No sign-in or sign-out, no Open blocker at close, and no Visit. An Observation attaches to the Shift its recorder is rostered on instead of to their Attendance. The person who saw the leaning fence post never loses the way to say so.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Horse records        | No profiles, Feed Schedules or Alerts, and so no per-horse Feed or Medicate Items. The Board, Medication Authority and per-horse Thresholds depend on it, so it cannot be switched off underneath them. Switching it on switches nothing else on.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

**Settings are changed by whoever holds `grants`, with an audit entry and a required reason, and never for one person.** When a gate is switched back on, it **flags** every Worker who now fails it rather than taking them off anything. This is ADR 0017's rule against emptying a roster, applied to a Setting rather than to a Release Version.

**A switched-off Feature is refused by the server as well as hidden.** Its writes answer with a sentence saying the Organisation does not use it, because navigation is never a boundary (ADR 0026). Its reads still answer, because what was recorded under it is still history.

**`bootstrap` takes a starting shape**: _rescue_ with everything on, which is today's behaviour, or _farm_ with only chores. A shape only sets the initial values. Nothing afterwards remembers which shape an Organisation started from. An Organisation that already exists changes its Settings in the admin screen like anybody else. `bootstrap --org` gains no way to set them, because that would be a second way to change a Setting that leaves no audit entry.

## Why this is not the override ADR 0011 refused

ADR 0011 rejected an override because of who uses it and when. A Coordinator blocked on one person, at the moment of need, sets the gate aside. Within a year, "assigned with override" is simply how newcomers get onto Saturdays. A Setting differs in what it is rather than in who may touch it. At a farm, one Owner holds every Scope, so the person blocked and the person holding `grants` are often the same. A Setting is a standing policy that applies to everyone at once. It cannot name a person, and every change to it carries a reason in the audit log.

**It is also the arming ADR 0017 asked for.** That ADR wanted the Release gate armed "by one deliberate human act when the backfill is declared complete — not by a config flag that lingers." Switching _Release required_ on is that act. What lingered there was an unarmed gate nobody had decided about. A Setting that is off has been decided, by a named person, with a reason. A farm that never asks its hands for a release has made a decision about its legal exposure. It has not quietly stopped enforcing a gate. The failure ADR 0011 feared still has a recognisable shape here: a Setting switched off on Tuesday morning and back on that afternoon, with one Worker rostered in between. The audit log is where that shows.

## Vocabulary that broke

Two of the glossary's words described the first rescue rather than the domain, and the second Organisation broke both.

- **Organisation** replaces _rescue_ as the general word, and **Worker** replaces _Volunteer_ as the person record. A Volunteer is now an unpaid Worker. That is still most people at a rescue, and still the people its Release and Consent were written about. Whether a Worker is paid is **not stored** until something reads it. Nothing yet behaves differently for a paid Worker, and a field nothing reads goes stale without anybody noticing. Renaming `volunteers` in the code is a separate decision.
- **Shift Types are named by each Organisation.** Feed AM, Feed PM and Lunch were one barn's words written into a constant. A farm's day may be one morning Job. Pop-up stays the one kind every Organisation has, because it is defined by how it is staffed. Holders of `roster` edit them. A rename changes the label in place, so everything already naming the type follows it. A type is Retired and never deleted, and retiring one is refused while a live Shift Pattern or a current Feed Schedule names it.
- **Owner** joins the fixed Role list with every Domain Scope, as President has. Roles do not become a list each Organisation writes, because a Role is authorization and ADR 0010 keeps authorization out of data. Screens otherwise say what the glossary says everywhere. There is no per-Organisation wording, since that would multiply every sentence the app writes.

## Chores

- **Organisation-wide Tasks.** A Task may be about the whole Organisation. That is where an animal Caballus keeps no record of lives: _Feed pigs_ is a Task, and the pigs are words in it. It is also where work off the farm lives.
- **Task order.** A Task carries a place in the order the Organisation wants its work listed. Per-horse cards keep stall order.
- **Items added by hand.** A holder of `roster` or `horse_care` (the same door as creating a Pop-up) may add an Item by hand to one dated Shift, such as the dead animal call that came in this morning. It names a catalogue Task and may carry **a note for this occurrence**, such as the address. The same Task may be added more than once, so two calls in one morning are two Items. The note is an instruction and never a chore's name, so the catalogue stays the only source of Tasks, and ADR 0013's free-text Pop-up item stays the only Item with no Task behind it.
- **Photographs.** A Task may ask for a photograph. The photograph is evidence attached to a Done and **never a condition of it**: the tick queues and lands as it does today. The photograph is shrunk to evidence size on the phone and queued behind the tick on its own. A photograph that has not uploaded yet shows on its Item but **does not block the Shift closing**: only work is Unsent, and a missing picture of work is not missing work. If the phone loses it, the Done stands. A dead camera or no signal must never stop somebody saying the pigs were fed. Photographs are kept as long as the record they are evidence for and read by whoever may read it. This is what forces the object storage #32 deferred.
- **Mobile-only Workers.** A Worker may be created from a mobile number with no email address, and sign in through Verify. Verify is platform-wide and needs no registration per Organisation (ADR 0029, ADR 0030). A Worker always keeps at least one credential: removing a mobile is refused when it is the only one, which ADR 0029 never needed because email was always there. **A Role needs an email address.** Escalations, close notes, the digest and Reorders reach Scope holders by email, and ADR 0028 keeps texts to two cases, so granting a Role to a mobile-only Worker is refused and names the missing address. Otherwise reports would be routed to somebody who silently never receives them.
- **The Boss.** The Boss is a Contact pinned to the Work Surface. Calling and texting them hand off to the phone's own apps. Caballus still carries no message between two people, and sends the Boss nothing when the job closes: he looks.

## Considered options

**An open table of feature flags.** This was rejected because every combination becomes a case each derivation has to answer, and an open table cannot be enumerated or tested. The list is closed. Only the values are data.

**A shape fixed per deployment, or a fork for farms.** This would share none of what a farm and a rescue have in common, which is nearly everything below the gates, and every fix would be made twice.

**A per-person override after all.** ADR 0011's reasons for refusing it are unchanged.

**Generalising Horse into Animal.** This is deferred rather than refused. Nothing in the sketch asks a question about a particular pig. A prospect who needs per-animal records for another species would reopen it.

## What would make this wrong

**A Setting toggled around a single person.** If the audit log shows a gate switched off and back on with one rostering in between, the Setting is being used as an override, and ADR 0011's reasoning applies to it directly.

**Combinations multiplying faster than the tests.** If a third Organisation needs a Feature split again, or Features start depending on each other in more than one direction, the closed list is turning into the flag table this ADR rejected. That is the time to stop and redraw the Features, not to add another.

**A chore list that needs a record.** If the farm starts asking which pig, which trailer, or which call, then Organisation-wide Tasks were standing in for an entity, and the Animal question comes back.
